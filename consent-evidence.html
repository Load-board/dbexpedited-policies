SMS Consent Language

This page reproduces the SMS consent clause from the Contractor Acknowledgment section of the DBExpedited LLC Independent Contractor Onboarding Packet, which every driver and dispatch team member signs before being enrolled in our internal operational SMS alert system.

As part of the Contractor Acknowledgment signed during onboarding, each enrolled driver and dispatch team member agrees to the following:

"I consent to receive operational SMS text alerts from DBExpedited LLC (including available freight load opportunities and schedule updates) at the mobile phone number I have provided. Message frequency varies based on freight load availability and may include multiple messages per day during active operating periods. Message and data rates may apply. I may opt out of receiving these alerts at any time by replying STOP to any message, or request help by replying HELP."

See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for further detail on how this SMS alert system operates.
