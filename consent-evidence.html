SMS Opt-In Evidence

DBExpedited LLC operates an internal operational SMS alert system for enrolled independent contractors (drivers and dispatch staff). This is not a public consumer marketing program — there is no web-based signup form. Enrollment happens on paper, during contractor onboarding, before any phone number is ever added to the alert system.

How a contractor opts in

Every independent contractor completes and signs our Independent Contractor Onboarding Packet before beginning work with DBExpedited LLC. The final page of that packet, the Contractor Acknowledgment page, is where SMS enrollment actually happens. On that single page, together in one place, the contractor:

  • writes the mobile phone number that will receive operational SMS alerts,
  • checks a standalone box consenting to receive those alerts, directly next to the full consent language (message frequency, data rates, and STOP/HELP opt-out instructions), and
  • signs and dates the page.

No phone number is ever added to the alert system without this completed, signed page on file.

Contractor Acknowledgment page (page 21 of 21) from the signed DBExpedited LLC Independent Contractor Onboarding Packet — the phone number field, SMS consent language, and signature line all appear together.

Note on the field visible above: the "Mobile Phone Number for SMS Alerts" line sits directly above the SMS Alert Consent paragraph and checkbox on the signed page, so the phone number being enrolled and the consent being given are shown together as part of the same signed record.

Consent language

"I consent to receive operational SMS text alerts from DBExpedited LLC (including available freight load opportunities and schedule updates) at the mobile phone number I have provided. Message frequency varies based on freight load availability and may include multiple messages per day during active operating periods. Message and data rates may apply. I may opt out of receiving these alerts at any time by replying STOP to any message, or request help by replying HELP."

For more detail on how contractor and operational data is handled, see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
