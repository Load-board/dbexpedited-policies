Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

DBExpedited LLC ("DBExpedited," "we," "us," or "our") operates an internal freight load-matching and alerting system used by our own drivers and dispatch personnel. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with that system, including SMS text message alerts.

Who This Applies To

This system and the associated SMS alerts are used exclusively by DBExpedited LLC personnel (drivers and dispatch/operations staff) for internal business purposes. We do not use this system to send marketing or promotional messages to customers or the general public.

Information We Collect

We collect and store the mobile phone numbers of DBExpedited personnel who are enrolled to receive operational SMS alerts. We also process freight load data (such as pickup/delivery locations, dates, and pay rates) obtained through FedEx Custom Critical's load board for the purpose of identifying and alerting relevant loads to our drivers.

How We Use Information

Phone numbers are used solely to deliver time-sensitive SMS alerts about available freight loads that match our operational criteria. We do not sell, rent, or share personnel phone numbers with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following third-party service providers to operate this system:

  • Twilio — to deliver SMS text messages
  • Google (Google Sheets) — to store and organize load and scheduling data
  • Anthropic — to assist with automated evaluation of load opportunities

These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually/technically limited to providing their respective services; they are not authorized to use the data for their own marketing purposes.

Message Frequency and Opt-Out

SMS alert frequency varies based on freight load availability. Message and data rates may apply. Enrolled personnel may opt out of receiving alerts at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance.

Data Retention

Phone numbers and related enrollment data are retained only as long as necessary to operate the alerting system for active personnel, and are removed upon request or upon a person leaving the organization.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we process, including restricting access to authorized personnel only.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact DBExpedited LLC at our business address in Scottsdale, Arizona, or via the contact information associated with this account.
